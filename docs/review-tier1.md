### What the runner got wrong

- **A pinned `ref_pick` survives a prompt re-roll.** dryad, medusa and sea-hag kept `ref_pick` 2 from the old strip; the re-roll drew a new strip and the mesh used its ref_2 blind (dryad's train, medusa's ring of snakes, sea-hag's seaweed stick). The other two candidates were better in two of the three. A prompt re-roll should drop (or ignore) the pin, or the review must re-pin before the mesh. medusa, giant-weasel and polar-bear now carry both a pin and a changed prompt: re-pin after their re-roll.
- **Staleness is evaluated live.** `status()` runs when the batch reaches a monster, so an override written during a run for a monster not yet reached changes the running pass (a prompt edit re-rolls it now). Edits in this review were made only after the batch passed the monster (black-dragon-wyrmling's was held back until then).
- **Ref timeout.** shambling-mound's ref was killed at 120 s (3 candidates; other refs took 22-50 s), leaving no candidates and the old pass-2 ref.png / mesh.
- **Unarmed check:** ogre ref_2 carries a pot in each hand at -0.41 (pots are not among its weapon words); not picked. satyr's auto pick 0 (2.47) has cords hanging from both hands, just under the 2.5 bar (re-picked).
