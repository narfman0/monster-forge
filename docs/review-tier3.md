## Systematic findings (tier 3)

1. **Unusual creatures are drawn as the nearest common thing (17 of 33).** otyugh an octopus, aboleth an eel, roper a cone bust, lemure a bodybuilder torso, marilith a two-armed woman on legs, xorn a horned quadruped, hydra a one-headed lizard, air / fire elementals and invisible stalker men in wind / fire rings (fire-elemental's disc backdrop meshed as a shell), will-o'-wisp a skull child in a bubble, awakened shrub / tree plain bonsai on soil, troll-limb a whole troll, crawling claws a photo hand, the rug a framed tapestry picture, plesiosaurus a short-necked crocodile. The registry prompts are short; the `prompt_extra` written here spells out the body. If a second round fails these belong in `prompt_tweaks.json` as per-id prompts (as chimera / medusa / ettin), and hydra / marilith (multi-limb) likely need a template like the multi-headed tier-2 records.
2. **The avian plan's wording grows wings on wingless bodies:** allosaurus 2 of 3, tyrannosaurus-rex 1 of 3 candidates have bat wings; wyvern 2 of 3 are four-legged dragons (wings PLUS forelegs). The plan entry needs an exception for the theropods (no wings) and the wyvern (wings are the arms, two legs).
3. **Ground and backdrops still mesh:** tarrasque and T. rex floor plates, soil mounds under the shrub and tree, troll-limb's grass patch, air-elemental's debris plate, fire-elemental's disc backdrop as a shell. `house_ground` covers floors but not a backdrop disc or a halo.
4. **Facing:** QA's insectoid `pca_upper_mass_end` faced giant-wasp, giant-centipede and remorhaz backwards (8 of 9 insectoids wrong across tiers 2-3: docs/review-tier2.md "Pass 5" finding 11); `pca_high_centre_end` faced seahorse backwards.
5. **NSFW:** marilith ref_1 is topless (never pick it); negatives raised in its `negative_extra`.
6. **Style drift:** crawling claws are photo-real (no ink style), ancient-brass and other metallic dragons rendered; the house style does not hold for hands and metallic subjects.
